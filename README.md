# 🎙️ Multi-Agent GD

**AI-Powered Group Discussion Practice Platform**

🔗 **Live Demo:** [https://multi-agent-gd.onrender.com](https://multi-agent-gd.onrender.com)

---

## 📌 What is this?

**Multi-Agent GD** is a web app that lets students practice **placement Group Discussions (GDs)** anytime, without needing friends or classmates.

You create a virtual discussion room, and **2–5 AI participants** (each with their own name, personality and speaking style) debate a topic with you. You speak using your **voice**, they respond naturally, and at the end you get a **detailed AI feedback report** on your communication skills.

> Think of it as a mock GD round that is available 24/7.

---

## ❓ The Problem

- Group Discussions are a major elimination round in campus placements.
- Students rarely get enough realistic practice, because arranging a GD needs several people at the same time.
- Even when they practice, they get no objective feedback on fluency, grammar, filler words or confidence.

## ✅ The Solution

AI participants that **talk to each other and to the user**, plus automated analysis of how the user performed.

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Create a Discussion**: pick a topic (or enter your own), language, duration, and configure 2–5 AI participants.
2. **Speak Naturally**: join the room and talk by voice. AI participants listen, respond, ask follow-ups and challenge your points.
3. **Get Feedback**: receive a performance report with scores and personalised improvement tips.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔐 Authentication

- User signup and login
- Secure JWT-based authentication

### 🗂️ Discussion Setup

- Predefined or custom topics
- Discussion mode selection
- Languages: **English, Hindi, Hinglish**
- Configurable duration
- 2–5 AI participants

### 🤖 AI Participants

- Custom names and gender
- Different personalities and speaking styles
- Natural AI-to-AI conversation
- Context-aware replies, follow-up questions and logical counter-arguments

### 🗣️ Discussion Room

- Voice-based discussion
- Live transcript
- Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech
- Speaking timer and pause detection

### 📊 AI Feedback Report

- Overall performance score
- Fluency and grammar evaluation
- Confidence score
- Vocabulary analysis
- Speaking speed
- Filler word detection
- Topic relevance
- Personalised improvement suggestions

### 📈 History & Profile

- Past sessions and feedback reports
- Practice statistics and average score

## 🎯 Key Highlights

- **Multi-agent design:** several AI personas converse with each other and with the user, not just one chatbot replying to you.
- **Voice-first:** a realistic speaking experience using browser-native speech APIs.
- **Actionable feedback:** metrics on fluency, grammar, confidence and filler words, not just a single score.
- **Multilingual:** supports English, Hindi and Hinglish, which suits Indian placement scenarios.
